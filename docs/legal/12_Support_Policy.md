# 1. Title
SpotUs Support Policy

# 2. Purpose
To inform users about the scope, availability, and expected response times for technical and account support for the SpotUs app.

# 3. Full Document

**Effective Date:** [Effective Date]

SpotUs is dedicated to providing reliable support for our users. This policy outlines what you can expect when you contact us for help.

### 1. Scope of Support
We provide support for:
- Account login issues (via our provider, Clerk).
- App crashes and technical bugs.
- Issues with location services and map rendering.
- Questions regarding billing (if applicable in the future).
- Safety and moderation reports (handled via the Safety Policy).

We do **not** provide support for:
- Retrieving messages from expired temporary rooms (these are permanently deleted).
- Resolving interpersonal disputes between users that do not violate our Community Guidelines.
- Issues related to your device's operating system or hardware.

### 2. How to Request Support
- **In-App:** Go to Profile > Settings > Report a Problem.
- **Email:** Send an email to [Support Email].

### 3. Response Times
Our support team operates during standard business hours. 
- **General Inquiries & Bugs:** We aim to respond within 48 to 72 business hours.
- **Safety & Moderation Reports:** As stated in our Content Moderation Policy, we review reports of objectionable content within 24 hours.

### 4. Required Information
When submitting a support request via email, please include:
- Your SpotUs username.
- The email address associated with your account.
- A clear description of the problem.
- Screenshots (if applicable).
*Do not send us your password.*
