# 1. Title
SpotUs Acceptable Use Policy

# 2. Purpose
To strictly define what technical and behavioral usage of the SpotUs platform is permitted and what is prohibited, focusing on system integrity and API abuse.

# 3. Full Document

**Effective Date:** [Effective Date]

This Acceptable Use Policy ("AUP") dictates how you may interact with the technical infrastructure of SpotUs. By using SpotUs, you agree to this AUP.

### 1. Prohibited Technical Actions
You agree that you will not:
- **Reverse Engineer:** Decompile, reverse engineer, or attempt to extract the source code of the SpotUs mobile application.
- **Scrape Data:** Use bots, scrapers, crawlers, or any automated tools to extract user data, location data, or messages from our Firebase database or API.
- **Location Spoofing:** Use GPS spoofing tools, VPNs designed to falsify location, or modified app clients to fake your location in order to join local rooms you are not physically near.
- **Denial of Service:** Engage in any activity that overloads, crashes, or attempts to crash our servers, Cloudinary storage, or Expo Notification systems.

### 2. Prohibited Content and Behavior
As reiterated in our Terms of Service and Community Guidelines, you may not use our infrastructure to:
- Distribute malware, viruses, or malicious code via chat rooms.
- Engage in phishing or attempt to steal the Clerk authentication credentials of other users.
- Exploit bugs or vulnerabilities in the app for personal gain or to harass others.

### 3. API Limits
We monitor API usage to prevent abuse. Creating an unreasonable number of rooms in a short period, or sending messages at a non-human rate, will result in automated rate-limiting and potential account suspension.

### 4. Enforcement
Violations of this Acceptable Use Policy will result in immediate termination of your account and, where applicable, civil or criminal prosecution.
