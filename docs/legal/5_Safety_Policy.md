# 1. Title
SpotUs Safety Policy

# 2. Purpose
To comprehensively outline the physical, emotional, and digital safety features built into SpotUs, and provide instructions on how users can protect themselves and report issues.

# 3. Full Document

**Effective Date:** [Effective Date]

At SpotUs, your safety is our top priority. Because our app connects you with people nearby, we have built specific features and policies to help you stay safe online and offline.

### 1. In-App Safety Features
- **Location Controls:** You have absolute control over your location sharing. You can choose to share your precise location, approximate location, or use "Ghost Mode" to hide your location entirely while browsing.
- **Blocking:** If someone makes you uncomfortable, you can instantly block them. Blocked users will no longer see your activity, and you will not see theirs. They cannot join rooms you create, and you will not be notified of their presence.
- **Reporting:** Every chat room and user profile features a "Report" button. Use this to alert our moderation team to harassment, hate speech, explicit content, or dangerous behavior.

### 2. Physical Safety When Meeting Locals
While SpotUs is meant for discovering local conversations, if you choose to meet someone from the app in person:
- **Meet in Public:** Always meet in a well-lit, public place.
- **Tell a Friend:** Let someone you trust know where you are going and who you are meeting.
- **Trust Your Instincts:** If a situation feels wrong, leave immediately.

### 3. Law Enforcement Cooperation
SpotUs cooperates fully with law enforcement to protect the safety of our users and the public. If we detect imminent threats to life or child exploitation, we will proactively report the activity to the relevant authorities.

### 4. Zero Tolerance Behaviors
We have zero tolerance for:
- Threats of real-world violence.
- Stalking or coordinated harassment.
- Child exploitation or grooming.
Engaging in these behaviors will result in an immediate, permanent ban and potential reporting to law enforcement.

### 5. Getting Help
If you are in immediate physical danger, contact your local emergency services (e.g., 911 or 112) immediately.
To report a non-emergency safety issue within the app, use the in-app reporting tools or email us at [Support Email].

# 5. In-App Summary (For Settings > Safety Center)
**Your Safety Toolkit:**
- **Control Your Location:** Use Ghost Mode to go invisible, or use approximate location if you don't want to share exact coordinates.
- **Block & Report:** Use the flag icon to report bad behavior, or block users to cut off all contact instantly.
- **Real-World Rules:** If you meet up with someone from a room, stay in public, tell a friend, and trust your gut.
- **In Danger?** If you are in immediate real-world danger, call local emergency services right away.
