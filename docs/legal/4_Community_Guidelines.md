# 1. Title
SpotUs Community Guidelines

# 2. Purpose
To establish the cultural norms and behavioral expectations for the SpotUs community, ensuring a safe, respectful, and engaging environment for local discovery.

# 3. Full Document

**Effective Date:** [Effective Date]

SpotUs is built on the idea of spontaneous, hyper-local connection. Because our platform is based on where you are right now, mutual respect and safety are our highest priorities. These Community Guidelines apply to everyone using SpotUs.

### 1. Be Respectful
SpotUs is for connecting with your local community—whether that's college students, festival-goers, or young professionals. Treat everyone with respect. Do not insult, bully, or harass other users. 

### 2. No Hate Speech or Discrimination
We have a zero-tolerance policy for hate speech. You may not attack, threaten, or discriminate against anyone based on race, ethnicity, national origin, religion, sexual orientation, gender identity, disabilities, or diseases.

### 3. Keep it Legal
Do not use SpotUs to conduct any illegal activities. This includes buying or selling illegal drugs, coordinating violence, sharing copyrighted material you do not own, or organizing scams.

### 4. No Explicit Content
SpotUs is a public square. Do not share pornographic imagery, sexually explicit content, or unsolicited sexual messages. 

### 5. Protect Privacy
Do not share someone else’s private information (doxxing), including their phone number, address, or precise real-time location without their explicit consent. 

### 6. Do Not Spam
Do not use temporary rooms to blast repetitive messages, self-promote excessively, or distribute malware/links to scams.

### 7. Use Ghost Mode Responsibly
Ghost Mode allows you to browse local rooms anonymously. Do not attempt to bypass Ghost Mode restrictions to harass users or manipulate the platform.

### 8. See Something, Say Something
If you see someone violating these guidelines, use the in-app Reporting tools. We rely on our community to help keep SpotUs safe.

**Consequences of Violating the Guidelines**
Violating these guidelines will result in actions ranging from a warning to a permanent, unappealable ban from the SpotUs platform.
