# 1. Title
SpotUs Privacy Policy

# 2. Purpose
To inform users about how SpotUs collects, uses, shares, and protects their personal data, including sensitive location data, in compliance with GDPR, CCPA, and App Store guidelines.

# 3. Full Document

**Effective Date:** [Effective Date]

Welcome to SpotUs ("we," "our," or "us"). SpotUs is a hyper-local social networking application ("App") designed to help you discover and participate in temporary location-based conversations.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our App and services. Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. By using SpotUs, you consent to the data practices described in this policy.

### 1. Information We Collect
We collect information you provide directly to us, information collected automatically, and information from third parties (such as Clerk, our authentication provider).
- **Account Data:** Name, Email address, Profile photo, and Username.
- **Location Data:** You may choose to share your precise location or approximate location to find nearby rooms. If you use Ghost Mode, your location is still used to show nearby rooms but your exact coordinates and identity are not broadcasted to others.
- **User-Generated Content:** Messages, images, and reactions sent within temporary chat rooms.
- **Device & Usage Data:** Information about your device, OS, IP address, and interactions with the App, which may be processed via Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics.

### 2. How We Use Your Information
We use your information to:
- Create and manage your account.
- Provide the core functionality of SpotUs (e.g., discovering nearby rooms and displaying local conversations).
- Facilitate communication between users.
- Enforce our Terms of Service, Safety Policy, and Community Guidelines.
- Prevent harassment, hate speech, bullying, and illegal activities.
- Send you message notifications, room activity alerts, and invitations via Expo Notifications.

### 3. How We Share Your Information
We do not sell your personal information. We may share your information with:
- **Service Providers:** Third parties that perform services for us, including Clerk (Authentication), Firebase Firestore (Database), Cloudinary (Image Storage), and Expo (Push Notifications).
- **Other Users:** Your username, profile photo, and user-generated content are visible to users in the same room. Your location is visible to others depending on your settings (Precise, Approximate, or Ghost Mode).
- **Legal Obligations:** If required by law, subpoena, or other legal process, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of SpotUs, our users, or others.

### 4. Temporary Rooms and Data Retention
SpotUs focuses on temporary interactions. Rooms expire automatically. While user-generated content is temporarily stored to facilitate the conversation, it is routinely purged from our active databases upon room expiration, in accordance with our Data Deletion Policy. We retain basic account data for as long as your account is active. 

### 5. Your Rights (GDPR & CCPA)
Depending on your location, you have the right to:
- Access the personal information we hold about you.
- Request the deletion of your personal information.
- Opt-out of the sharing of your personal information.
- Request correction of inaccurate data.
To exercise these rights, please contact us at [Support Email].

### 6. Location Permissions and Ghost Mode
You have full control over your location permissions. You can disable location tracking at the OS level at any time. When using "Ghost Mode," you can browse public activity without broadcasting your location or participating in rooms.

### 7. Children's Privacy
SpotUs is intended for users aged 16 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under the age of 16. If we become aware that a user under 16 has provided personal information, we will immediately delete that account and data.

### 8. Changes to this Privacy Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Effective Date" and, where required, sending you an in-app notification.

### 9. Contact Us
If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at [Support Email].

# 5. In-App Summary (For Settings > Privacy Policy)
**Privacy at SpotUs - The Short Version:**
- **What we collect:** Name, email, profile photo, username, and your location (precise or approximate, depending on your choice). We also collect the messages and images you send in rooms.
- **How we use location:** To show you nearby rooms and let you connect with locals. You can turn this off or use Ghost Mode to browse invisibly.
- **Temporary by design:** Chat rooms expire automatically. Your messages aren't meant to live forever.
- **Who we share it with:** We do not sell your data. We share it only with our essential service providers (like our database and login providers) to make the app work.
- **Your Control:** You can delete your account, disable location, and request your data at any time.
