# 1. Title
SpotUs Child Safety Standards Policy

# 2. Purpose
To explicitly outline our protections for minors, compliance with COPPA (Children's Online Privacy Protection Act), and zero-tolerance approach to child exploitation.

# 3. Full Document

**Effective Date:** [Effective Date]

SpotUs is dedicated to protecting the safety and privacy of minors on our platform. 

### 1. Age Restrictions
SpotUs requires all users to be at least 16 years of age. Our platform is not directed at, nor do we knowingly collect personal information from, children under 16. If we discover that a user is under 16, their account and all associated data will be deleted immediately.

### 2. Zero Tolerance for Child Exploitation
SpotUs has a zero-tolerance policy for child sexual abuse material (CSAM), grooming, or any form of child exploitation.
- **Immediate Action:** Any account found sharing, requesting, or discussing such material will be permanently banned immediately.
- **Reporting to Authorities:** We actively report instances of CSAM and grooming to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) and relevant law enforcement agencies globally.
- **Evidence Preservation:** In such cases, we preserve relevant account and interaction data as required by law to assist in investigations.

### 3. Parental Controls and Advice
While SpotUs is for ages 16+, parents of 16-17 year olds should be aware that the app uses location services to connect users with nearby strangers. 
- Parents can use OS-level controls (iOS Screen Time, Android Family Link) to restrict access to the app.
- Parents can disable location services for the SpotUs app in the device settings, which severely restricts the app's functionality but ensures location privacy.

### 4. Reporting Child Safety Concerns
If you encounter a child safety issue on SpotUs, please report the user in-app immediately using the "Child Safety" reporting category, or email our specialized trust and safety team at [Support Email] with the subject line "URGENT: Child Safety".
