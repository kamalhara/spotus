# 1. Title
SpotUs Account Deletion Policy

# 2. Purpose
To provide users with clear instructions on how to permanently delete their account and understand the consequences of doing so, as required by Apple App Store guidelines.

# 3. Full Document

**Effective Date:** [Effective Date]

SpotUs gives you full control over your account. If you no longer wish to use SpotUs, you can permanently delete your account at any time.

### 1. How to Delete Your Account
You can initiate account deletion directly within the SpotUs app:
1. Go to your **Profile**.
2. Tap the **Settings** icon.
3. Scroll to the bottom and tap **Delete Account**.
4. Confirm your decision.

Alternatively, you can request account deletion by emailing us at [Support Email] from the email address associated with your SpotUs account.

### 2. What Happens When You Delete Your Account
Upon confirming account deletion:
- Your Clerk authentication profile is permanently deleted.
- Your Firebase Firestore user profile (name, username, photo) is erased.
- You will be immediately logged out, and you cannot reactivate your account.

### 3. What Happens to Your Content
Because SpotUs relies on temporary rooms, any active rooms you created will be immediately deleted, kicking out any current participants. Messages you sent in other active rooms will remain until those rooms expire, but they will no longer be associated with your profile (they will appear as sent by a "Deleted User").

### 4. Data Retention After Deletion
While your profile is deleted immediately from active view, certain administrative records (e.g., your past consent to our Terms of Service) may be retained for up to 30 days in our backups before being fully wiped. If your account was banned for violating our Safety Policy, we may retain your email address or device identifier permanently to prevent you from returning to the platform.

# 5. In-App Summary (For Settings > Delete Account)
**Account Deletion Notice:**
- Deleting your account is **permanent**. You cannot undo this.
- Your profile, name, and photo will be erased.
- Any active rooms you created will be deleted instantly.
- Messages you sent in other people's active rooms will show up as from a "Deleted User" until that room naturally expires.
