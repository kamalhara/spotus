# 1. Title
SpotUs Content Moderation Policy

# 2. Purpose
To explain how SpotUs monitors, reviews, and acts upon user-generated content to maintain compliance with App Store and Google Play guidelines for User-Generated Content (UGC).

# 3. Full Document

**Effective Date:** [Effective Date]

SpotUs relies on a combination of automated systems and community reporting to enforce our Community Guidelines and ensure a safe environment.

### 1. What We Moderate
We moderate user-generated content, which includes:
- Text messages sent in public and private rooms.
- Images uploaded to chat rooms.
- User profile data (Profile Photos and Usernames).
- Room titles and descriptions.

### 2. How We Moderate
- **Automated Filtering:** We employ automated keyword filters to detect severe hate speech, explicit terms, and spam. Content flagged by these filters may be blocked from being sent or flagged for immediate human review.
- **Community Reporting:** Users are our strongest line of defense. Every piece of user-generated content has a "Report" button. 
- **Action SLA:** SpotUs commits to reviewing reports of Objectionable Content within 24 hours of submission.

### 3. Actions Taken on Violations
When a violation is confirmed, our moderation team will take one or more of the following actions:
- **Content Removal:** The offending message, image, or room will be immediately deleted.
- **Temporary Suspension:** The user's account may be temporarily suspended from creating rooms or sending messages.
- **Permanent Ban:** For severe violations (e.g., child exploitation, explicit imagery, hate speech, severe harassment), the user will be permanently ejected from the platform.

### 4. Appeals Process
If your account has been suspended or banned, and you believe it was in error, you may appeal the decision by contacting [Support Email]. Our team will review the appeal and make a final determination within 7 business days. Note that bans for child exploitation or severe threats are unappealable.

### 5. Objectionable Content Definition
As mandated by App Store Guidelines, "Objectionable Content" includes, but is not limited to: offensive, insensitive, upsetting, intended to disgust, or in exceptionally poor taste material. SpotUs actively filters and removes this content.
